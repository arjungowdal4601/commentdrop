Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

What CommentDrop does

CommentDrop lets YouTube creators send a file (for example a PDF) by email to viewers who comment a keyword on their video, and helps creators understand and reply to their comments.

Creators

When you continue with Google, we store your Google account email and name, your YouTube channel's name, ID and picture, and a Google refresh token. We use them only to: read comments on your videos, post the replies you confirm, and send material emails from your Gmail address to viewers who asked for them. Files you upload are stored to be sent to those viewers.

When you use the Comments page, the comments on the chosen video and its title and description are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to produce the sentiment analysis, groups and suggested replies.

Viewers

When you link your YouTube account, we store your email and your public YouTube handle, name and picture, so a creator's material can reach you when you comment their keyword.

What we don't do

We don't sell data, show ads, or read your Gmail. The Gmail permission is used only to send material emails. Google user data is handled according to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
